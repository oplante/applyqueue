Live packages

The card with a date inside the next 10 working days rises. A later date sits below. A date that has passed stays at the top until you change it.

What this page does

Four roles on one page: Dates, Evidence, Draft and Hold. It prepares the firm’s own application figures from the rate card and the evidence already on the card.

It does not chase invoices, read a contract, build a PDF pack, or register anyone.

ApplyQueue is not legal advice. It does not certify a payment. It does not interpret a contract. Empty fields stay empty.

Standing rules

Only a date already written on a card is used — a note is not a deadline, and a certificate expected date does not move a package up the queue.

Nothing is guessed: no rate, no price; no evidence, no draft figure. A needed-and-missing item parks the line. Nothing is sent, by anyone, ever, from this page.
